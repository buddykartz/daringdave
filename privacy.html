Privacy Policy

Daring Dave · Effective 28 September 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy
In short: Daring Dave does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect personal data such as your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage analytics, and it contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game, Daring Dave saves your settings, level progress, high score and Daily Challenge results only on your device. This information is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is deleted if you delete the app.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and saving images

When you tap Share on a Daily Challenge result, the game creates an image and a short text on your device and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is uploaded by the game; you choose where it goes. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks your permission to add the image to your Photos. The game only adds that image — it cannot read your photo library.

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It has no chat, no accounts, no ads and no links out of the game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
