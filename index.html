Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock the other worlds?

Green Hills (9 levels) is free. A single one-time purchase, Unlock All Levels, unlocks Desert Ruins, Frozen Peaks and Molten Core — 45 more levels and 3 boss fights. Tap UNLOCK ALL on any locked world in Select World. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

I bought Unlock All Levels, but the worlds are locked on my new device

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with, then open Select World and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Purchases can't be moved to a different Apple ID.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is the Daily Challenge?

A new level every day — the same one for every player. Beat it as fast as you can with 3 hearts, keep your daily streak going, and share your result card with friends. It's free for everyone.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Everything, including the Daily Challenge, works offline. An internet connection is only needed to buy or restore the unlock.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Deleting the game deletes your level progress, high score and daily streak (your purchase is kept by Apple and can be restored). Settings → Reset Progress clears progress but keeps purchases.

The game feels slow or my phone gets warm

Open Settings → Graphics and choose Medium or Low.

The on-screen buttons are in the way

In Settings you can switch the controls to left-handed and change the button opacity.

Is it safe for kids?

Daring Dave has cartoon-style action only, no ads, no chat, no accounts and no links out of the game. It doesn't collect any personal data — see our Privacy Policy.
